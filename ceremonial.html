Ceremonial Matcha

A term commonly used to describe high-quality matcha intended for drinking on its own. These matchas are often valued for their vibrant appearance, fine texture, and balanced flavor.

ABOUT THE MATCHA

What makes it unique?

Ceremonial-grade is a commonly used term for matcha that is considered suitable for drinking on its own, particularly when prepared as usucha, or thin tea.

Matcha in this category is often finely milled and valued for characteristics such as vibrant green color, smooth texture, umami, and natural sweetness. However, the term itself does not have a single official grading standard, so quality can vary between producers.

TASTING NOTES

A closer look.

AROMA

Fresh & Vegetal

Often associated with fresh, grassy, vegetal, or subtly sweet aromas.

TASTE

Umami & Sweet

A well-balanced matcha may have noticeable umami alongside a natural sweetness.

FINISH

Smooth & Lingering

Higher-quality drinking matcha can have a smooth mouthfeel with a lingering flavor.

PREPARATION

How can you enjoy it?

Ceremonial-style matcha is commonly prepared as usucha and enjoyed on its own. Sift the powder first, add a small amount of water, and whisk until the tea becomes smooth and develops a layer of fine foam.

You can adjust the amount of matcha and water according to your preferred strength and taste.
