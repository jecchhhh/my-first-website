Culinary Matcha

Matcha commonly described as culinary grade is intended for use in food and beverages. Its stronger flavor allows it to remain noticeable when combined with other ingredients.

ORIGIN Japan
GRADE Culinary-style
FLAVOR Bold · Earthy · Vegetal
COLOR Green
TEXTURE Fine Powder
BEST FOR Lattes · Desserts · Baking

ABOUT THE MATCHA

Made to stand out.

Culinary-style matcha is commonly used as an ingredient rather than being reserved for drinking on its own.

Its more pronounced flavor can work well with milk, sugar, chocolate, fruits, and other ingredients. It is frequently used in matcha lattes, cakes, cookies, ice cream, sauces, and other desserts.

The term "culinary grade" is not an official standardized grading system, and the quality and characteristics of matcha can differ between producers.

TASTING NOTES

A closer look.

AROMA

Earthy & Grassy

A noticeable vegetal and grassy aroma that can remain present when combined with other ingredients.

TASTE

Bold & Vegetal

A stronger matcha character that can complement milk, sugar, chocolate, and other flavors.

FINISH

Distinct & Lasting

The matcha flavor can remain noticeable after being combined with other ingredients.

HOW TO USE IT

Beyond the bowl.

Culinary-style matcha can be incorporated into a variety of recipes. Try it in matcha lattes, smoothies, cakes, cookies, ice cream, puddings, or other desserts.

When adding matcha to a recipe, sift the powder first to reduce clumps and help distribute it evenly throughout the mixture.
