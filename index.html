ABOUT THIS JOURNAL

More than
just a green powder.

Matcha has a long history rooted in Japanese tea culture. This website is a personal collection exploring different matcha powders, their origins, flavors, and characteristics.

Each entry is created to make learning about matcha simple, visual, and enjoyable.
