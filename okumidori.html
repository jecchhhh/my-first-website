Okumidori Matcha

01 / ABOUT THE MATCHA

A naturally vibrant cultivar.

Okumidori is a tea cultivar developed in Japan and is widely appreciated for its bright green appearance and smooth character. Its name is associated with its deep green color, which makes it particularly attractive when prepared as matcha.

Compared with some stronger and more astringent matcha, Okumidori is often described as having a softer and more balanced profile, making it approachable for both traditional preparation and modern matcha drinks.

02 / TASTING NOTES

A closer look.

AROMA

Fresh & Vegetal

TASTE

Sweet & Umami

Smooth umami balanced by a gentle natural sweetness.

FINISH

Clean & Smooth

A soft finish with relatively gentle bitterness and lingering green tea notes.

03 / PREPARATION

Let the color shine.

Okumidori can be enjoyed as traditional usucha, where its vibrant color and smooth flavor can be appreciated on their own. It also works well in modern drinks such as matcha lattes.

For a simple bowl, sift about 2 grams of matcha into a bowl, add around 60–70 ml of warm water, and whisk until a fine, foamy layer forms on the surface.
