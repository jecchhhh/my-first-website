04 · JAPAN

Okumidori Matcha

Okumidori is a Japanese tea cultivar known for its beautiful deep green color and smooth, balanced character. It is often appreciated for its gentle sweetness and pleasant umami.

01 / ABOUT THE MATCHA

The deep green cultivar.

Okumidori is a tea cultivar developed in Japan and is particularly recognized for its rich green color. The cultivar is commonly used in the production of matcha and other Japanese green teas.

Its name is associated with its deep green appearance. Okumidori is valued for producing matcha with a smooth character, moderate astringency, and a pleasant balance of sweetness and umami.

The final character of Okumidori matcha can still vary depending on where and how the tea was grown, the harvest, shading period, processing, and the producer.

03 / PREPARATION

Simple, smooth, and versatile.

Okumidori can be enjoyed traditionally as usucha, allowing its vibrant color, sweetness, and umami to stand out. Sift about 2 grams of matcha into a bowl and add approximately 60–70 ml of warm water.

Whisk the matcha with a bamboo chasen using quick, back-and-forth movements until the powder is fully mixed and a light layer of foam appears.

Because of its smooth and balanced character, Okumidori can also be used in matcha lattes, smoothies, and desserts. Its vibrant color makes it especially appealing in preparations where the appearance of the matcha matters.
