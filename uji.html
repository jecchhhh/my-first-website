01 · UJI, KYOTO, JAPAN

Uji Matcha

Uji Matcha comes from Uji in Kyoto, one of Japan's most recognized tea-producing regions. It is known for its refined character, vibrant green color, and rich umami.

ORIGIN Uji, Kyoto, Japan
CULTIVAR Varies by producer
FLAVOR Umami · Sweet · Vegetal
COLOR Vibrant Green
TEXTURE Fine · Silky
BEST FOR Usucha · Koicha · Lattes
01 / ABOUT THE MATCHA

From the heart of Kyoto.

Uji is a historic tea-producing area in Kyoto, Japan, with centuries of association with Japanese tea. The region's reputation comes from its long tea-growing tradition and the careful cultivation and production methods used by local tea producers.

Uji Matcha is traditionally made from shade-grown tea leaves that are processed into tencha before being finely ground into powder. The shading process helps develop the tea's characteristic umami and vibrant green appearance.

Because Uji Matcha describes tea associated with a region rather than one single cultivar or grade, its exact flavor, color, and quality can differ depending on the producer, cultivar, harvest, and processing methods.

03 / PREPARATION

The traditional bowl.

Uji Matcha can be prepared as usucha, a lighter and more commonly enjoyed style of matcha. Sift about 2 grams of matcha into a bowl and add approximately 60–70 ml of warm water.

Using a bamboo whisk called a chasen, whisk the matcha quickly in a back-and-forth motion until the powder is fully incorporated and a light layer of foam forms on top.

Matcha is generally prepared with water below boiling temperature. Water that is too hot can make the drink taste more bitter and astringent.
